---
title: A knowledge graph shared beyond its authors needs added context
summary: Notes that make sense to close collaborators do not carry enough context for outsiders, so sharing is a step of its own.
---

Researchers who share [discourse graphs](../../entities/discourse-graphs/) found that a working graph has enough context for close collaborators only; to show it to a wider community they have to prepare a subset of the pages with more context, and some share results inside their lab first ([Protocol Labs, 2023](../../sources/discourse-graphs-future-of-science/)). The [llm-wiki](../../entities/nvk-llm-wiki/) plugin likewise exports a project's knowledge as a separate checkpoint with privacy minimization and recorded omissions, rather than handing over the wiki itself ([nvk, n.d.](../../sources/nvk-llm-wiki/)).

**Inference:** a research written by an agent from zero, with its question on the card and every claim tied to a source page, carries more of its context than a personal graph does, which lowers the cost of sharing it. Sharing still is a step: a colleague who continues it [forks it](../forking-lets-divergent-versions-coexist/) rather than editing the original.

---
title: A research built only from public posts needs no privacy filter
summary: Keeping private material out of the agent's reach, rather than asking the agent to filter it, is what lets each research be public by default.
---

Andy Smith concluded that an agent working from private notes would let something private slip into a public wiki sooner or later, so his auto-researcher works only from notes he has already chosen to publish ([Smith, 2026](../../sources/andy-smith-auto-researcher/)). He wants the agents to lift his posts to known models or theories and then read those theories back through his writing, producing results he would want to read ([Smith, 2026](../../sources/andy-smith-social-media-to-learn/)).

**Inference:** when the inputs are public, a research can be public from its first commit, and a private research is an explicit exception chosen at the start rather than a filter applied at the end. A research that [starts from zero](../one-topic-per-wiki-is-established-practice/) also cannot pull private context from another research, because it reads others only as [published, pinned versions](../depending-on-a-pinned-version-decouples-researches/).

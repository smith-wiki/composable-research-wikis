---
title: Curating by choosing whole researches scales where curating pages does not
summary: When each research is a frozen unit and a combined wiki is a view, curation becomes choosing which units the view includes instead of editing pages.
---

The tools that isolate topics also let a person act on a topic as a whole. The [llm-wiki](../../entities/nvk-llm-wiki/) plugin archives a whole topic wiki, hiding it from queries and research, and restores it later ([nvk, n.d.](../../sources/nvk-llm-wiki/)). A [Federated Wiki](../../entities/federated-wiki/) neighborhood is whatever sites a reader has chosen to visit ([Udell, 2015](../../sources/federated-wiki-neighborhoods/)).

**Inference:** agents write pages far faster than anyone reads them, so page-level curation of an agent-written wiki loses the race ([accumulation on shared pages](../pages-every-run-edits-accumulate-until-they-mean-nothing/)). Choosing units does not: a reader judges a research by its card, keeps it in a combined wiki, or drops it, and dropping it is exact because the combination is a [view](../composing-wikis-as-a-view-makes-merges-reversible/). A bad research is discarded whole rather than cleaned, and a good one stays as it was published.

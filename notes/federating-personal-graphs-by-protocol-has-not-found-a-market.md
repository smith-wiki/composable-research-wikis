---
title: Federating personal knowledge graphs through a new protocol has not yet found a market
summary: Noosphere built a protocol joining public and private sub-graphs into one graph, and its company wound down without product-market fit.
---

[Noosphere](../../entities/noosphere/) was designed as one large multiplayer knowledge graph made of smaller public and private sub-graphs, built on content addressing, with the aim of connecting personal knowledge graphs ([Brander, 2022](../../sources/noosphere-protocol-for-thought/)). In 2024 its company, Subconscious, wound down active operations: the team had built a working multiplayer graph protocol for notes but did not find product-market fit ([Brander, 2024](../../sources/subconscious-winding-down/)).

**Inference:** the failure was of the market, not of the technique, so it is weak evidence against composable wikis and strong evidence against building them as a new protocol. Composing researches from Git repositories, links, and a static site generator, as [Dendron](../../entities/dendron/) and [llm-wiki](../../entities/nvk-llm-wiki/) do, needs no one else to adopt anything; see [composing wikis as a view](../composing-wikis-as-a-view-makes-merges-reversible/).

---
title: Keeping each research area in its own wiki is established practice
summary: LLM wiki tools, note-taking tools, and Luhmann himself split knowledge into independent collections instead of growing one.
---

Splitting a knowledge base by area is not new. The [llm-wiki](../../entities/nvk-llm-wiki/) plugin keeps a hub with no content of its own and one isolated topic wiki per research area, each with its own indexes, so that queries stay focused and one topic's pages do not add noise to another's ([nvk, n.d.](../../sources/nvk-llm-wiki/)). [Dendron](../../entities/dendron/) lets one workspace hold several vaults, each of which can be its own Git repository and can be kept private or published separately ([Dendron, 2022](../../sources/dendron-multi-vault/)). [Niklas Luhmann](../../entities/niklas-luhmann/) kept his roughly 90,000 cards in two largely separate collections, the first kept from about 1952 to 1960 and the second from 1961 to early 1997, rather than one growing box ([Niklas Luhmann-Archiv, n.d.](../../sources/luhmann-zettelkasten/)).

**Inference:** a research that starts from zero in its own repository follows this practice; what the tools add is a way to look across the collections without merging them, described in [composing wikis as a view](../composing-wikis-as-a-view-makes-merges-reversible/). Isolation has a cost, which [isolating researches gives up](../isolation-gives-up-the-gains-of-compiled-shared-knowledge/) states.

## Overview

This German-language archive page describes Niklas Luhmann's physical Zettelkasten, its history, contents, card types, arrangement, numbering, references, and keyword access. The Niklas Luhmann Archive provides it for researchers and readers who need an institutional account of the collection and its digitization. It is a detailed, authoritative finding aid based on the surviving archive, though the page flags uncertainties where the original order or dates cannot be reconstructed. Among these sources, it supplies the clearest mature example of keeping a large research collection usable: stable addresses, local branching, selective cross-links, separate bibliographic material, and indexes provide entry points without imposing a fixed global hierarchy.

## Key points

- The roughly 90,000 cards are divided between two largely separate collections: Zettelkasten I has about 22,000 cards, and Zettelkasten II has about 67,000. (Section 3.1, "Umfang und Inhalt")
- Zettelkasten I was mainly created from about 1952 to 1960, while Zettelkasten II spans 1961 to early 1997. (Section 3.1, "Umfang und Inhalt")
- Each collection has its own bibliography section; Zettelkasten II later expanded to individual bibliography cards, with about 18,000 literature cards in all when reused card backs are counted. (Section 3.2(b), "Bibliographiezettel")
- Keyword indexes were the central access tool: Zettelkasten I's index has about 1,250 entries, and the largest Zettelkasten II version has about 3,200. (Section 3.2(c), "Schlagwortregisterzettel")
- Every card receives a permanent address; letters and further digits branch new cards between existing ones without renumbering them. (Section 3.4, "Nummerierungsprinzip")
- Card numbers create direct, structural, distant, sometimes bidirectional, and collective references that connect related material across local sequences. (Section 3.5, "Verweisungsprinzip")
- The keyword index supplies selected entry points rather than exhaustive matches, relying on the internal reference network for further discovery. (Section 3.6, "Schlagwortregister")

## Overview

This brief 2026 blog post sketches Andy Smith's idea for using his own social-media accounts as a learning surface. Aimed at readers following his experiments with agents, automation, and writing, it proposes having agents generalize informal posts into known models or theories and then reinterpret those theories through Smith's other writing. The desired test is personal usefulness: the transformed results should be material he genuinely wants to read, with possible value to others as a consequence. The source is reliable for Smith's intent and proposed workflow, but it reports an idea rather than an implemented or evaluated system. It relates to his auto-researcher by describing another reuse path for a public corpus.

## Key points

- Smith proposes that agents generalize his posts into a known model or theory before producing social-media material. ("they generalize my writing up to some known model or theory")
- The agents would then examine those theories through the perspective of Smith's own writing. ("they look at those theories through my own writing")
- The primary success criterion is that Smith himself would want to read the transformed and automatically researched results. ("I'd actually want to read the results")
- He suggests that material interesting to him may also interest other people. ("if I find it interesting, other people probably will too")
- Smith flags platform compliance and clear AI-generated labeling as questions still to resolve. ("I need to check how well it lines up with the platforms' rules")

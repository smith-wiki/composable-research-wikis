question: "How do people organize research wikis so that they do not rot into a dump within days, and can independent research wikis be shared, merged, and unmerged? Survey existing systems and derive a design where one research is one repository."
brief: "Give every research its own repository, freeze it on publish, and combine researches only as views you can drop. Agent wikis rot because every run edits shared pages and coins names; tools that last isolate topics and merge by viewing, not editing."
date: 2026-09-24
---

## Answer

Wikis that agents maintain rot because every run rewrites shared pages and invents names, faster than anyone can review: the first Smith Wiki grew several pages per idea ([accumulation](notes/pages-every-run-edits-accumulate-until-they-mean-nothing/), [synonyms](notes/agents-that-name-things-freely-produce-synonym-clusters/)).

Tools that stay usable [keep one topic per wiki](notes/one-topic-per-wiki-is-established-practice/) and [combine wikis as views](notes/composing-wikis-as-a-view-makes-merges-reversible/), so dropping one undoes the merge. A merge that also unifies content needs [immutable, identified, attributed units](notes/a-unifying-merge-needs-immutable-units-identifiers-and-provenance/). Sharing works by [forking](notes/forking-lets-divergent-versions-coexist/), with [added context](notes/a-graph-shared-beyond-its-authors-needs-added-context/).

The design: one research, one repository, [public by default](notes/research-built-only-from-public-posts-needs-no-privacy-filter/) and [served at its own path](notes/one-domain-can-serve-every-research-at-its-own-path/); newer researches cite [pinned versions](notes/depending-on-a-pinned-version-decouples-researches/) of older ones; curation means [choosing whole researches](notes/curating-by-selection-scales-where-curating-pages-does-not/). The cost is [lost reuse](notes/isolation-gives-up-the-gains-of-compiled-shared-knowledge/); the warning is [Noosphere](notes/federating-personal-graphs-by-protocol-has-not-found-a-market/): build on Git and static sites, not a new protocol.

---
title: Discourse graphs
summary: A note model in which short questions, claims, and evidence are linked into a graph, used by research labs to share their state of knowledge.
---

A discourse graph labels each brief note as a question, a claim, or evidence and links them; labs such as Matt Akamatsu's share one graph among their members ([Protocol Labs, 2023](../../sources/discourse-graphs-future-of-science/)).

It appears in [sharing needs added context](../../notes/a-graph-shared-beyond-its-authors-needs-added-context/).

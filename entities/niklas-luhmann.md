---
title: Niklas Luhmann
summary: German sociologist whose card index of about 90,000 linked slips is the model for the Zettelkasten method.
---

Niklas Luhmann kept his notes on cross-linked cards in two largely separate collections, each with its own bibliography and keyword index. Every card has a permanent address, new cards branch between existing ones without renumbering, and the keyword index offers a few selected entry points rather than every match ([Niklas Luhmann-Archiv, n.d.](../../sources/luhmann-zettelkasten/)). The notes of this research follow his slip form: one claim per page, a permanent path, links to the notes it belongs with, and no concept pages beside them.

He appears in [one topic per wiki](../../notes/one-topic-per-wiki-is-established-practice/) and, through permanent addresses, in [a unifying merge needs stable identifiers](../../notes/a-unifying-merge-needs-immutable-units-identifiers-and-provenance/).

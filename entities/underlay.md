---
title: Underlay
summary: A Knowledge Futures project for publishing knowledge graphs as immutable, attributed assertions reduced into a materialized state.
---

Underlay's ARC protocol builds a knowledge graph from assertions, immutable RDF datasets with provenance, reduces them into a materialized state, and groups them in collections with a schema ([Underlay, n.d.](../../sources/underlay-arc-protocol/)).

It appears in [a unifying merge needs immutable units](../../notes/a-unifying-merge-needs-immutable-units-identifiers-and-provenance/).

---
title: llm-wiki (nvk)
summary: An open-source agent plugin that builds LLM-compiled knowledge bases as a hub of isolated topic wikis.
---

llm-wiki is a plugin for Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, and other agents that turns Andrej Karpathy's LLM wiki idea into commands for research, ingestion, compilation, querying, and auditing ([nvk, n.d.](../../sources/nvk-llm-wiki/); [Karpathy, n.d.](../../sources/karpathy-llm-wiki/)). It is the closest existing tool to one research per wiki: a hub holds no content, each topic is its own wiki with isolated indexes, whole topics can be archived and restored, a read-only portfolio looks across them, and one project's knowledge can be exported as a checkpoint and imported elsewhere as evidence.

It appears in [one topic per wiki](../../notes/one-topic-per-wiki-is-established-practice/), [composing wikis as a view](../../notes/composing-wikis-as-a-view-makes-merges-reversible/), [curating by selection](../../notes/curating-by-selection-scales-where-curating-pages-does-not/), [pinned versions](../../notes/depending-on-a-pinned-version-decouples-researches/), and [sharing needs added context](../../notes/a-graph-shared-beyond-its-authors-needs-added-context/).

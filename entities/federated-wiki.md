---
title: Federated Wiki
summary: Ward Cunningham's wiki in which every user has a site, pages are forked between sites, and a reader's neighborhood of sites is searched and linked as one.
---

In Federated Wiki, editing another person's page copies it onto your own site with its journal of edits, and the original author may take the changes back or not ([Cunningham, 2012](../../sources/on-federating-wiki/)). The sites a reader visits form a neighborhood whose pages can be linked by title and searched together ([Udell, 2015](../../sources/federated-wiki-neighborhoods/)).

It appears in [forking](../../notes/forking-lets-divergent-versions-coexist/), [composing wikis as a view](../../notes/composing-wikis-as-a-view-makes-merges-reversible/), and [curating by selection](../../notes/curating-by-selection-scales-where-curating-pages-does-not/).

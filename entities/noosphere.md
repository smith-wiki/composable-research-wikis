---
title: Noosphere
summary: Gordon Brander's protocol for a multiplayer knowledge graph of public and private sub-graphs, behind the Subconscious notes app.
---

Noosphere joined personal knowledge graphs, called spheres, into one graph through content addressing ([Brander, 2022](../../sources/noosphere-protocol-for-thought/)). Its company, Subconscious, wound down in 2024 without product-market fit ([Brander, 2024](../../sources/subconscious-winding-down/)).

It appears in [protocol-level federation](../../notes/federating-personal-graphs-by-protocol-has-not-found-a-market/).

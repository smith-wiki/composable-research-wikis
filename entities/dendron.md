---
title: Dendron
summary: A Markdown note-taking tool for VS Code whose workspaces combine several vaults, each of which can be its own repository and private or published.
---

Dendron organizes notes in vaults. A workspace opens several vaults at once, a vault can be a separate Git repository, and vaults marked private are left out of publishing ([Dendron, 2022](../../sources/dendron-multi-vault/)). A 2022 proposal lets a vault declare the vaults it depends on, stored under their origin and version ([Dendron, 2022](../../sources/dendron-self-contained-vaults/)).

It appears in [one topic per wiki](../../notes/one-topic-per-wiki-is-established-practice/), [composing wikis as a view](../../notes/composing-wikis-as-a-view-makes-merges-reversible/), [pinned versions](../../notes/depending-on-a-pinned-version-decouples-researches/), and [protocol-level federation](../../notes/federating-personal-graphs-by-protocol-has-not-found-a-market/).
